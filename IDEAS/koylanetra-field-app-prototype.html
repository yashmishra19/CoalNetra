Try this order

1. Open Continue round and complete Bench 4. Mark one check as Not OK and watch it open a capture already filled in.

2. Switch Network off. Record an observation with a voice note and a photo. The queue counter rises and nothing is lost.

3. Switch Network back on and watch the queue drain in the strip and in the Sync locker.

4. Finish the round, open Report, sign it and submit. The prescribed form is produced for the paper register as well.

Why it is shaped this way

The overman must file a shift report every shift under Regulation 47(7)(d) of the Coal Mines Regulations 2017, and hand over conditions to the next official. The report is the spine of this app, so observations and readings arrive as a by-product of work that cannot be skipped.

Every target is at least 56dp for gloved hands, voice comes before typing, and no risk score or ranking appears anywhere. Underground mode disables GPS and photographs and states that an intrinsically safe device approved by DGMS is required.
